# Lender Underwriting & Eligibility Specifications

This document outlines the detailed underwriting guidelines, credit boxes, hard-stop exclusions, and programmatic criteria parameters for the 3 integrated lenders: **SmartBiz Bank**, **Breakout Capital**, and **Idea Financial**.

---

## 1. SmartBiz Bank (SBA Programs)

SmartBiz offers two primary SBA 7(a) small business bank loan products: **SBA Streamline** and **SBA Traditional**.

### Program Comparison & Credit Box

| Parameter | SmartBiz SBA Streamline | SmartBiz SBA Traditional |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loan Amount Range** | $50,000 – $150,000 | $150,001 – $350,000 |
| **Pricing / Rate** | Prime + 5.75% (Prime + 4.5% for 10yr TIB / 190+ Liquid Credit) | Prime + 3.00% |
| **Packaging & Closing Fees** | $2,500 + ~$500 closing; 1.7% guarantee fee (≤$150k) | $2,500 + ~$500 closing; 2.25% guarantee fee (>$150k) |
| **Use of Proceeds** | Working capital, debt refinance, equipment purchase, supplier inventory | Working capital, debt refinance, equipment purchase, supplier inventory |
| **Minimum FICO** | **680** minimum for all guarantors | **660** (weighted average) |
| **SBSS / Liquid Credit** | 170 Etran minimum (175–190 depending on sector/TIB) | 170 Etran minimum (175–190 depending on sector/TIB) |
| **Time in Business (TIB)** | **3 years** minimum (10 years for lower credit ratio) | **3 years** minimum (10 years for lower credit ratio) |
| **Liquidity Ratio (LCR)** | 2.0x – 3.5x | 2.0x – 3.0x |
| **Min DSCR** | n/a | **1.0x** Business and Global (tax return + interim) |
| **Max Loan-to-Revenue** | **40%** of last filed tax return revenue | **50%** of last filed tax return revenue |
| **Average Daily Balance (ADB)**| **$15,000+** strongly recommended (<$6k auto-declined) | **$15,000+** strongly recommended (<$6k auto-declined) |
| **NSF / Overdraft Tolerance**| Max **3 NSFs/Overdrafts** in last 6 months | Max **3 NSFs/Overdrafts** in last 6 months |
| **MCA / Factoring Rules** | **Hard Stop**: Ineligible if any MCA/Factoring line is outstanding on debt schedule | **Hard Stop**: Ineligible if any MCA/Factoring line is outstanding on debt schedule |
| **Max Tax Installment Debt** | Total installment balance must be **≤$20,000** | Total installment balance must be **≤$50,000** |

### NAICS / Industry Ineligibility (Hard Stops)
- All SBA general ineligible industries (gambling, adult, lending, speculation, etc.)
- `2362`: Nonresidential Building Construction
- `4243`: Apparel, Piece Goods, and Notions Merchant Wholesalers
- `4251`: Wholesale Trade Agents and Brokers
- `4411`: Automobile Dealers
- `4492`: Electronics and Appliance Stores
- `4841`: General Freight Trucking
- `4842`: Specialized Freight Trucking
- `4853`: Taxi and Limousine Service
- `721`: Accommodations (Hotels/Motels)

### Public Records & Background
- **Federal Tax Liens**: **Zero** allowed outstanding (regardless of age).
- **Local / State Liens**: None > $5,000.
- **Bankruptcy**: Max 1 bankruptcy seasoned ≥ 3 years. For Traditional: Lifetime limit of 1 filing per guarantor.
- **Judgments / Litigation**: No judgments > $5,000; no active litigation.
- **Criminal**: No Felony convictions (certain misdemeanors allowed).
- **Credit Seasoning**: Chargeoffs/repos > 18 months seasoned; Foreclosures > 3 years seasoned; No settlements > $500 in last 12 months; No collections > $500; No currently late accounts.
- **Ownership/Guarantor**: All owners & key employees must be US Citizens or LPRs. Ineligible: 401(k) plans, ESOPs, COOPs.

## 2. Breakout Capital (Term Loans & Working Capital)

Breakout Capital offers two core programs: **Breakout Prime** and **Breakout Waive**.

### Program Comparison & Credit Box

| Parameter | Breakout Prime | Breakout Waive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum Funding** | Up to **$1,000,000** | Up to **$250,000** |
| **Loan Terms** | 6 to 24 months | 12 months |
| **Repayment Frequency** | Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly | Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly |
| **Minimum FICO** | **600+** | **600+** |
| **Time in Business (TIB)** | **1 year** | **1 year** |
| **Min Monthly Revenue** | **$50,000** ($600,000 annualized) | Consistent cashflow |
| **Position / Payoffs** | 1st Position (will consolidate up to 2 positions; payoffs up to $500,000) | Zero net policy |
| **Early Prepayment Benefit** | Standard 25% interest forgiveness on prime; 100% interest forgiven on renewal | **100% remaining interest forgiven** if repaid after 30 days |
| **Credit Inquiry** | Soft credit pull | Soft credit pull |

### State Restrictions (Hard Stops)
- **Restricted States**: **CA, NV, SD, ND, MT, RI, VT**

### Entity & Industry Restrictions
- **Trucking Companies**: Hard restriction if fewer than 10 registered trucks.
- **Sole Proprietorships & General Partnerships**: Ineligible.
- **Financial Institutions**: Banks, credit unions, lenders, bail bonds, factoring, debt buyers, credit repair.
- **Vehicle / Equipment Dealers**: Auto dealers, RV & trailer, boat dealers, mobile home dealers.
- **Vice / High Risk**: Adult entertainment, gambling/lotteries, horoscope/fortune telling.
- **Government & Non-Profits**: Ineligible.
- **Farming & Agriculture**: Ineligible.
- **Specialty Sales**: Pawn shops, MLM, travel clubs, flea markets, precious metals.
- **Documentation Thresholds**:
  - Loans > $150,000 require business tax returns.
  - Loans > $250,000 require full financial statements (P&L and Balance Sheet).

## 3. Idea Financial (Line of Credit & Term Loans)

Idea Financial provides flexible business lines of credit and term financing for established operations.

### Credit Box & Minimum Qualifications

| Parameter | Minimum Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Time in Business (TIB)** | **3 years** minimum (Construction requires **7+ years**) |
| **Minimum Monthly Revenue** | **$15,000** ($180,000 annualized) |
| **Minimum FICO** | **650** |
| **Minimum Ownership** | **50%** cumulative guarantor ownership required |
| **Average Bank Balance (ADB)** | **$5,000** minimum monthly average balance |
| **NSFs / Overdrafts** | Max **6 in any single month**, max **24 in past 6 months** |
| **Negative Days** | Max **3 negative days in a month**, max **9 in past 6 months** |
| **Bank Deposits** | Minimum **8 deposits per month** |
| **Existing Positions** | Maximum **2 current positions** (underwriter discretion if payoff needed) |
| **Bankruptcies / Foreclosures** | None within the last **3 years** of disposition date |
| **Entity Types** | **No sole proprietorships**, **no non-profits** |

### Geographic Scope
- Funds nationally **except**: **Vermont (VT)**, **North Dakota (ND)**, **South Dakota (SD)**.

### Restricted Industries (Hard Stops)
- Farming and mining
- New home construction and residential remodeling (Note: general commercial construction requires 7+ years TIB)
- Used cars and vehicle dealerships
- Telecommunications / media publishing
- Real estate investment / rental properties
- General government support
- Travel agencies
- Natural gas and power generation / distribution
- Gun stores and firearms
- Oil, gas, air, water, and rail transportation
- Banking, securities, and commodities investments
- Elementary schools and performing arts
- Cannabis cultivation and dispensaries

### Preferred Industries
- Health Care and Social Assistance (medical clinics, pharmacies, veterinarians)
- Retail Trade (grocery, convenience, meat & fish markets)
- Manufacturing (parts, medical devices, consumables)
- Landscaping
- Wholesale Trade
- Law firms
- Accommodation & Food Services (established restaurants, breweries)
